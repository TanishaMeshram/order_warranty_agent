STORE RETURN, REFUND AND WARRANTY POLICY

RETURN POLICY

Electronics: eligible for return within 7 days from the purchase date.
Clothing: eligible for return within 15 days from the purchase date.
All other products: eligible for return within 7 days from the purchase date.

WARRANTY POLICY

Electronics: 365 days from the purchase date.
Clothing: 30 days from the purchase date.
All other products: 30 days from the purchase date.

RETURN CONDITIONS

Products must be unused and in acceptable condition.
The customer should provide valid proof of purchase.
Items that are damaged due to misuse may not be eligible for return.
The final return eligibility is determined using the purchase date and applicable product category policy.

REFUND POLICY

Approved returns are eligible for a refund according to the store's refund process.
Refund processing time may vary depending on the payment method.

SHIPPING POLICY

Orders are shipped after payment confirmation.
Customers can check their order status using their Order ID.
